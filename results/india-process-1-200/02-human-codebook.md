# 02 - The principal investigator's codebook, organised

His tagged export holds **342 code-segment pairings** over
**342 segments**. Organising them by the first hyphen gives
**15 top-level codes** and **116 sub-codes**,
131 in all; 117 of them are leaves (a top-level code with no sub-codes
is a leaf as well as a family).

Nothing here was invented by the pipeline: the tree is his labels, read back.

## Definitions, and where they came from

**131 of 131 codes carry a definition.** Their stated source, as the
organiser recorded it code by code:

| description source | codes |
|---|---:|
| `pi-codebook-md` | 131 |

`../Grounded AI Futures/codebook/AI_Perceptions_Codebook1.md` is the document they were read from;
`../Grounded AI Futures/data/CodebookIndiaProcess(1-20)CodesExamples.csv` is the export the pairings came from.

**A definition is only as shareable as whoever wrote it made it.** Each one was
tested on its own against every response in the corpus before being reproduced here:
**40 of 131** share a 20-character run with a response and
appear below as *definition withheld*, with their code name intact. That is the
exporter's own guard applied per item rather than to the file as a whole — a
definition that quotes the response which prompted it is respondent text, however
it is labelled.
